# Commercial Loan Policy – BFSI Lending Division
Version 2.1 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025

## 1. Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum credit score: 650
- Business operating for at least 3 years
- Annual revenue must be at least 1.5x the requested loan amount for working capital
- Clean repayment history on existing loans (no defaults in last 5 years)
- KYC verification must be completed before loan processing
- All required documents must be submitted and verified

## 2. Loan Types and Limits
- **Working Capital**: $100K – $5M, tenure 12–48 months
- **Term Loan**: $500K – $25M, tenure 24–120 months
- **Equipment Finance**: $50K – $2M, tenure 12–84 months
- **Trade Finance**: $100K – $3M, tenure 6–24 months

## 3. Interest Rate Guidelines
- Base Rate: 7.25% (Prime Rate)
- Working Capital: Base + 1.0% to 3.5%
- Term Loan: Base + 0.75% to 2.5%
- Equipment Finance: Base + 1.5% to 3.0%
- Trade Finance: Base + 2.0% to 4.0%
- Premium for high-risk applicants: +2.0% to +4.0%

## 4. Approval Authority
- Up to $500K: Branch Manager approval
- $500K – $2M: Regional Credit Head approval
- $2M – $10M: Credit Committee approval
- Above $10M: Board Credit Committee approval

## 5. Collateral Requirements
- Working Capital: 125% collateral coverage
- Term Loan: 150% collateral coverage
- Equipment Finance: Equipment itself serves as primary collateral
- Trade Finance: Trade documents / Letter of Credit

## 6. Documentation Checklist
### Mandatory Documents
1. Financial Statements (last 3 years, audited)
2. Bank Statements (last 6 months)
3. Tax Returns (last 3 years)
4. Business License / Certificate of Incorporation
5. Identity Proof of Directors/Partners
6. Collateral Documents (valuation report)

### Optional Documents
- Business plan
- Cash flow projections
- Existing loan statements

## 7. Processing Timeline
- KYC Verification: 1–2 business days
- Document Review: 2–3 business days
- Risk Assessment: 1 business day
- Credit Committee: 2–3 business days
- Final Approval: 1 business day
- Disbursement: 2–3 business days
- **Total Standard Processing: 9–13 business days**

## 8. Rejection Criteria
- Credit score below 650
- Debt-to-income ratio above 55%
- Incomplete or falsified documentation
- AML/KYC failure
- Default history in last 5 years
- Business in prohibited industries
