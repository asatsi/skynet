# KYC (Know Your Customer) Policy – AML Compliance
Version 3.0 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025

## 1. KYC Objectives
- Prevent money laundering and financial fraud
- Verify customer identity and legitimacy of business
- Ensure regulatory compliance (BSA/AML, FATCA, FinCEN)
- Maintain accurate customer risk profiles

## 2. Customer Due Diligence (CDD) Levels
### Standard CDD (all customers)
- Business registration verification
- Director/partner identity verification
- PAN/Tax ID verification
- Address verification
- Beneficial ownership verification (>25% ownership)

### Enhanced Due Diligence (high-risk customers)
- Source of funds documentation
- Business activity review
- Enhanced background check
- Senior management approval required
- Ongoing monitoring (quarterly)

## 3. Required Verifications
### PAN/Tax ID Verification
- Cross-check against government database
- Name must match exactly
- Address must match within tolerance

### Address Verification
- Utility bill not older than 3 months
- Bank statement with address
- Government-issued address proof

### Bank Verification
- Cancelled cheque or bank letter
- Must match company name
- Account must be active

### Compliance Screening
- OFAC sanctions list check
- PEP (Politically Exposed Persons) screening
- Adverse media search
- AML transaction pattern review

## 4. KYC Re-Verification Schedule
- Standard customers: Every 2 years
- Medium-risk customers: Every 1 year
- High-risk customers: Every 6 months
- Event-triggered: Change of directors, address, ownership

## 5. KYC Failure Actions
- Immediate loan suspension
- Escalation to Compliance Officer
- SAR (Suspicious Activity Report) if required
- Customer blacklisting for severe cases

## 6. Record Retention
- KYC records: 7 years after relationship closure
- Transaction records: 5 years
- SAR filings: 7 years
